# Data governance and scope

## Included research material

The `gold/` directory contains evaluation-only, act-level transcriptions and derived structured records from nineteenth-century civil registers. These records preserve uncertainty, literal-script readings, and provenance fields. They are not a definitive index and should not be treated as one.

## Source material

Source scans are not redistributed in this repository. The project records source references and artifact checksums where available so a researcher can independently verify a record against the source collection. Users are responsible for following the access, citation, and reuse terms of the holding archive.

## Rights and privacy

The included records are historical and concern deceased people. AKT Reader's runtime refuses records inside a configurable privacy window by default. This does not replace institutional review, archive terms, or a researcher's own legal and ethical assessment.

## Training and evaluation

The gold corpus is evaluation-only. It must not be used for training or tuning unless a separate, documented consent and rights review establishes that use. The repository does not include source scans, private reviewer traces, or unreviewed labels.

## Limits

AKT Reader is an evidence-management and extraction toolkit. It does not resolve identity, establish kinship, or make historical claims. Any downstream interpretation requires human review of the original record and appropriate domain expertise.
